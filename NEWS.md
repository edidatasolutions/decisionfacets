# decisionfacets 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Many-facet Rasch rating scale model fitting via 'TAM' (marginal ML) or built-in JMLE (`df_fit()`).
* Explicit decision rules: raw total, fair average, measure (`df_cut()`, `df_classify()`).
* Counterfactual pass probabilities under observed, average and random rater panels, with rater-dependence flags (`df_counterfactual()`).
* Attribution of expected misclassification to measurement error and rater assignment (`df_attribute()`).
* Known-truth simulation (`df_simulate()`).
